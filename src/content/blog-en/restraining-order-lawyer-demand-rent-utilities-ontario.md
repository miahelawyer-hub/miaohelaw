A client came in for advice. The house is in her name only. Her husband and their children live in it. A restraining order forced her to move out, but the mortgage and the property tax are still in her name, and she still pays them. She wants her husband to pay rent and utilities. But the restraining order says she cannot contact him.

The restraining order restricts you. It does not restrict your lawyer.

## 1. What a restraining order is

In Ontario there are two kinds. A family court restraining order is a civil order under section 46 of the [Family Law Act](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90f03). A judge makes it when one spouse has reasonable grounds to fear for their safety. It can bar direct or indirect contact, and keep the person away from the home, the workplace, or the children's school. A criminal no-contact order is a condition of release in a criminal case, under section 515 of the [Criminal Code](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-46/). It bars the accused from contacting the complainant. Both kinds bind the person named in the order. Both carry serious consequences for breach. A breach of a family restraining order can be prosecuted under section 127 of the Criminal Code. A breach of a bail condition is a new criminal charge.

---

## 2. The restraining order restricts you, not your lawyer

A no-contact condition binds the person named in it. It does not bind that person's lawyer. Some restraining orders say no direct or indirect contact. That still binds only you. Hiring a lawyer is not indirect contact. A lawyer writing to your spouse, or to your spouse's lawyer, about the rent, the utilities, the mortgage, and the separation is lawful communication about separation matters, not personal contact. Many restraining orders already say so in writing. They include an exception for contact through a lawyer. Indirect contact means something else. It means getting other people to pressure your spouse for you, or sending people under your control to reach them, putting them back at risk. That is what the restraining order forbids. What you must not do is contact him yourself. Do not call, text, or message him on [WeChat](/en/blog/electronic-evidence-ontario-family-court/). Do not ask a friend or a relative to pass on a message. That is a breach, and it can be charged.

---

## 3. If the restraining order is too broad, it can be changed

Some restraining orders are written very widely. They bar all contact of any kind, or keep a person away from a home they own, with no exception for legal matters. That is not always the end of the story. A criminal lawyer can apply to the criminal court to vary the terms, for example to allow contact through a lawyer, or contact for parenting and property matters. The Crown's position matters, and the court decides. The family side is separate. A family lawyer handles the separation, the property, the rent, and the support. We have handled cases like this. The two cases move in different courts, but they affect each other, so they need to be coordinated.

---

## 4. Send the correspondence letter whether you are divorcing or not, and even if he will not pay

The correspondence letter establishes a record. He is not paying rent, so the claim needs to be on record, with dates and amounts. The letter proves you asserted your rights promptly instead of letting the claim sit. That matters. The rent claim is what lawyers call [occupation rent](/en/blog/house-appreciation-after-separation-ontario/), the compensation the spouse living in the home may owe the spouse who moved out. When a court decides it, one of the factors the judge weighs is when you started claiming the rent. A spouse who asked early is in a stronger position than a spouse who waited years and then asked. The amounts add up too. Every month he lives in the house without paying rent or utilities is another month on the claim. The letter does not guarantee payment. It guarantees a record.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. If I message him on WeChat myself to ask for money, is that a breach?

Yes. The restraining order bars direct and indirect contact. A text or a WeChat message from you is direct contact, even if it is only about money. Let your lawyer send it. That is what the lawyer is for.

### 2. What if he does not answer the letter?

The letter has already done its job. It is on the record. Non-payment does not erase the claim. The amounts keep adding up, and the claim goes into the family case, where the court sorts out who paid what and who owes what.

### 3. Does the restraining order affect the divorce case?

The two cases are separate, but they affect each other. The criminal court controls the no-contact order. The family court decides the separation, the property, and the support. Your family lawyer and your criminal lawyer need to coordinate, so that what happens in one case does not hurt you in the other.
