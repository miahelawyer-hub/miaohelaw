No. Once a divorce agreement is filed with the civil affairs bureau in China, it is final. The bureau will not accept a revised version, even one both spouses signed. Anything left out cannot be added later. An agreement signed without a divorce certificate has no effect. Canada takes the opposite approach. A [separation agreement](/en/blog/separation-agreement-vs-divorce-ontario/) becomes binding the moment both spouses sign it.

A client of mine divorced in China several years ago. She and her husband already had children by then, but the divorce agreement never mentioned custody. The parents were in agreement about the children, so neither of them saw any reason to put it in writing. The problem surfaced years later. The children needed to move their household registration. The local police station would not process the move without proof of custody, and the only proof it would accept was the divorce agreement. The agreement said nothing about custody. Both parents were willing to sign a new agreement covering custody, but the civil affairs bureau refused to accept one. A private understanding between the parents cannot replace the agreement on file. Once a divorce agreement is filed in China, it cannot be changed.

## 1. A divorce agreement in China has to be complete before it is filed

A divorce agreement in China has to be complete before it is filed. Custody, child support, household registration, property, and debts should all be settled in writing while the agreement is still a draft. Once the agreement is filed, it is final. A term left out at that stage cannot be added later. Couples in the middle of a divorce usually want the process over with as quickly as possible, and when they already agree about the children, putting it in writing can feel unnecessary. In China, that is the kind of omission that causes real trouble later.

---

## 2. Without the divorce certificate, the agreement has no effect

A second client ran into a different problem. She and her husband had signed a divorce agreement in China and believed the divorce was complete. It was not. The couple had never gone back to collect the divorce certificate, and without that certificate, the agreement had no legal force at all. In China, a divorce agreement becomes effective only when the divorce certificate is issued. A signature on the agreement is not enough. Until the certificate is issued, the couple is still married, and the agreement means nothing.

---

## 3. A Canadian separation agreement binds the moment both spouses sign it

Canada handles this differently. There is no such document as a divorce agreement in Canada. The equivalent is a [separation agreement](/en/blog/separation-agreement-vs-divorce-ontario/), and it becomes binding as soon as both spouses sign it. No divorce certificate is needed for it to take effect. A separation agreement can also be amended. If the spouses agree to a change, they can sign an [amending agreement](/en/blog/separation-agreement-validity-ontario/). The divorce certificate is a separate court document, and it does only one thing. It confirms that the marriage has ended. It contains nothing about children, support, or property. The two systems operate in opposite directions, and applying the rules of one to the other is how mistakes happen.

---

## 4. Check every item on the list before you sign

Before signing a divorce agreement, it is worth checking every item on the list: who has custody of the children, where the children will live, how much child support will be paid, where the household registration will be, who keeps the home, and how savings and debts are divided. Every item should appear in the agreement. A short agreement may look clean, but it is often short because something was left out.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. Both spouses agree to the change. Can we sign a new divorce agreement to add the missing terms?

No. In China, the agreement filed with the civil affairs bureau cannot be changed. The spouses can come to their own understanding, but the filed agreement remains unchanged, and government offices will only recognize the version on file.

### 2. We signed a divorce agreement but never collected the divorce certificate. Is the agreement valid?

No. In China, a divorce agreement becomes effective only when the divorce certificate is issued. Without the certificate, the couple is still married, and the agreement has no legal effect.

### 3. Does a separation agreement in Canada become effective only after the divorce certificate is issued?

No. Canada has no divorce agreement. A separation agreement becomes binding as soon as both spouses sign it. The divorce certificate is a separate document that only confirms the marriage has ended.
